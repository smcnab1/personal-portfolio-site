## Context

Writing a useful clinical simulation scenario takes time: the educator has to align learning outcomes, patient information, cues, resources and progression.

## Problem

Generative AI can help with a first draft, but unstructured prompting risks producing generic or clinically unhelpful material. The tool still needs a clear educational workflow and expert review.

## Role

I built SimuGen as an open experiment and learning project, informed by my work in simulation education and healthcare AI.

## Approach

The project explores structured inputs and outputs for scenario generation. It treats AI as a drafting aid for educators, not as an authority on clinical content or a replacement for professional judgement.

## Current status

SimuGen is publicly available as an open-source project. It also informed presentation work on AI-driven simulation scenario generation.

## Methods

- Generative AI prototyping
- Structured scenario design
- Web development
- Human review and educational judgement
