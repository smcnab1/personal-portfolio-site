## Context

Simulation educators need more than a collection of techniques. They need a coherent way to connect educational theory, scenario design, facilitation, technology and evaluation.

## Problem

Professional development in simulation can become fragmented across short courses and local practice. A postgraduate programme needs to respect experienced practitioners while helping them examine and strengthen the reasoning behind their work.

## Role

I provide course leadership for the MSc Simulated Practice Education at the University of West London, alongside module leadership, teaching and personal tutoring.

## Approach

The curriculum treats simulation as an educational practice rather than a set of devices. It connects design, facilitation, debriefing, immersive technology, evidence and reflective practice.

## Current status

This is ongoing programme leadership. Confidential university material and learner information are deliberately not included here.
