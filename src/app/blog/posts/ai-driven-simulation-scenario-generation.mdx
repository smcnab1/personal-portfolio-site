Generative AI can make the blank-page stage of scenario design faster, but speed is not the same as educational quality.

This presentation explored a structured approach to using AI for first drafts while keeping learning outcomes, clinical review, inclusivity and educator judgement at the centre. The focus was practical: where the technology helps, where it does not, and what a responsible workflow needs around it.

The ideas also informed [SimuGen](/work/simugen), an open software experiment in structured scenario generation.
