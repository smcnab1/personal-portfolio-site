Some students are described as not fitting education when the more useful question is whether education was designed to fit them.

This presentation examined learning through the perspective of a neurodivergent educator. It focused on practical, neuro-affirming choices: making expectations visible, reducing unnecessary cognitive load, offering legitimate ways to participate and designing support into the default experience.
