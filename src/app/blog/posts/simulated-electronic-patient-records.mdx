Electronic patient records are part of contemporary nursing practice, but learners need a safe way to develop digital confidence before using live clinical systems.

This conference contribution described the application of simulated electronic patient records within undergraduate mental health nursing education. The work connected digital documentation with realistic learning activity while avoiding real patient data.

The implementation is explored further in the [SimEPR case study](/work/simepr).
