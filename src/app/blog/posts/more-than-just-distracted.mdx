ADHD in higher education is often reduced to distraction or time management. That misses the interaction between executive function, sensory load, motivation, communication and the systems around a person.

This presentation connected lived experience with teaching practice. It considered how apparently small design choices can create repeated friction, and how clearer structures, meaningful flexibility and fewer hidden expectations can improve learning without lowering standards.
